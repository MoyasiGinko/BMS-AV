# Product Story (source of truth for intent)

A complete SaaS **Business Management Platform**: one connected system replacing separate tools for sales, inventory, purchasing,
accounting, CRM, HR/payroll, reporting and communication. Multi-tenant: a business registers, picks a plan, configures its
organization (branches, departments, warehouses), invites employees and gets a working digital workspace immediately.

Connectedness is the point: products -> purchasing -> inventory -> sales/POS -> invoices -> payments -> accounting; customers ->
sales/CRM; employees -> HR/attendance/payroll -> expenses/accounting; managers -> dashboards/reports/analytics. A sale automatically
affects inventory, financial records, the customer balance and reports. A purchase raises stock and creates supplier + accounting
records. Payroll flows into expenses and accounting.

Tenancy: isolated users, roles, permissions, branches, warehouses, data and configuration on shared infrastructure. Plans enable
modules: small shop = POS+Sales+Inventory+Customers; growing = +Purchasing+Accounting+CRM+HR+Payroll; large = full ERP + multi-branch +
advanced analytics + automation + integrations + AI.

Owner dashboard answers: sold today, fastest products, what is low, who owes us, what we owe, where expenses rise, best branches,
employee performance, financial position, what needs attention today.

Long-term: Business Management System -> ERP -> Business Operating System -> intelligent platform with automation and an AI assistant
("Why did profit drop this month?", "Which products should we reorder?", "Customers who haven't bought in 90 days", "Create a PO for
products that need restocking") acting with the user's data, permissions and business rules.

Philosophy: **One business. One platform. One connected system.**
